Musical Chairs

A feast in the spirit of "Does anyone remember where I sat?"

A whole evening of good food, games, laughs, and people you haven't met yet. You don't choose who to sit with, the table chooses for you. And right at the perfect moment, everyone switches to the next table, with a new game and new people.

How it works

  1. 1

    Come solo, with friends, or with a partner. On average 70% come alone, and we make sure everyone feels at home from the first minute.

  2. 2

    Get a table. Each table has a fun social game waiting, to be finished within 20 minutes.

  3. 3

    When time's up, get up and mix. Move to the next table, with new people and a new game.

What's on the tables

Every dinner has a slightly different mix of games. A few examples:

  • Invent an outrageous dish (or drink), convince the kitchen or the bar to make it, and send it to another table with an anonymous dedication.
  • Find as many things as you can that everyone at the table has in common, and try to beat the other tables.
  • Guess surprising facts about your tablemates.

Who it's for

Anyone who wants an evening of pure fun, interesting conversations, witty games, good food and total escapism.
