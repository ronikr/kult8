What we do

We design connection experiences for festivals, retreats, conferences, communities, and more. The format adapts to the people, the setting, and the time available — from a 45-minute workshop to a full evening of structured social experiences.

Our events and workshops are built around one simple idea: people connect better when the environment is designed for it. Sometimes the goal is meeting potential partners. Sometimes it's making new friends. And sometimes it's simply getting a room full of strangers to talk to each other.
